GroundWorks Owner Guide

GroundWorks does not have one right way to run a shop. This guide explains what each part does and describes setups that work well for many shops. Take what fits yours and skip the rest. The screenshots use an example shop called Sunrise Coffee and were taken on a computer.

Start here

Pick a setup that fits your shop

There are a few ways shops use GroundWorks. None is the intended one. Read these three, then decide which is closest, or blend them.

Setup A

Count the back

Your team taps − when a unit leaves the storeroom for the floor. The count means "what is in the back". Open and on-the-floor stock is not tracked.

  • Often fits: shops that enter stock by hand, or that reorder from back stock.
  • Plan: Standard or Pro.
  • Upkeep: a few taps per shift and a closing count.
Setup B

Let sales do it

Recipes plus the Square connection. The app takes ingredients off the count as drinks sell. The count means "everything on hand, back and floor". The closing count is the safety net.

  • Often fits: shops on Square that want the least daily work.
  • Plan: Pro.
  • Upkeep: deliveries and a closing count, daily or weekly.
Setup C

A mix

Sales sync handles the drink ingredients (milk, beans, syrups, cups). Your team counts everything else by hand, such as stir sticks, napkins and to-go bags.

  • Often fits: shops where some items never appear in a recipe.
  • Plan: Pro.
  • Upkeep: list which items are which, so nothing is counted twice.

Your call.

Whatever you choose, tell your team. Part 1 of the training guide has a box for exactly this, so baristas know which items they tap and which the app handles. Counting the same item both ways is the one thing that reliably throws counts off.

Step 1

Add your items

On the Update tab, tap + New item. Editing an item later uses the same fields, plus a Delete button.

Item name

Whatever your team calls it. If you will use recipes, matching the name your register uses saves work later.

Counted in

The unit you count and order by: gallon, carton, bag, bottle, sleeve, box.

Category

Perishables, Dry Goods & Syrups, or Disposables. They only group the list. Pick the closest fit.

Starting count

What you have right now, in that unit. Decimals are fine, like 2.5.

Reorder level

The point where the item turns amber, lands on the Order tab and sends a restock text. At half of it, the item turns red and sends another.

Amount per unit and Measure

Optional. They say what a unit holds, for example 128 oz in a gallon. You only need them for recipes (Pro) or the Measure view.

Suggestion Start small.

Many shops begin with the 15 to 25 things that stop service when they run out: milk, beans, cups, lids and syrups. Add the rest once the habit is in place.

Suggestion Setting reorder levels.

One common approach: roughly what you use between deliveries, plus a cushion for a busy week. If you go through six gallons of milk between deliveries and a late truck is a risk, a level of 3 or 4 gives you room. Watch the Order tab for two or three weeks, then adjust. Levels can be decimals.

Can't delete an item?

If a recipe uses it, GroundWorks keeps it so your recipes do not break. Remove it from those recipes first.

Step 2 · Optional

Expiry dates for fresh items

Fresh items have an Expiry button. When a delivery arrives, your team can choose Add with expiry date, so each delivery carries its own date. The one that expires first is marked "Use first", and sales or counts draw from it before newer stock.

Suggestion Worth it for dairy and short-dated items.

It adds a step at delivery time, so many shops use it only where waste is a real cost. Items without expiry dates behave exactly as before.

Step 3

Text alerts

  • Restock texts go out when an item gets worse, for example from Stocked to Reorder Soon, or Reorder Soon to Critical. One text per step, with no repeats while it sits at the same status.
  • Expiration texts go out a set number of days before a batch expires, once per batch. The default is 3 days, and you can change the number on the page.
  • Who gets them is whoever you add. Type a number any normal way, like (555) 234-5678. Outside the US, start with + and the country code. Each person ticks the consent box, and can reply STOP to opt out.
Your call.

Some shops text only the owner. Others add a manager or an opening lead so the right person sees it first. There is no limit that you must use.

Step 4 · Optional

Owner PIN

Many shops leave a shared login on a counter tablet. An owner PIN lets your team count, log use and receive deliveries without being able to change settings. Skip it if your team is small and trusted.

What it protects

  • Locked with a PIN: editing and adding items, Recipes (including Square), Alerts and billing.
  • Always open: tapping − and +, deliveries, expiry dates, the closing count and the Order tab.

How to turn it on

  1. Scroll to the bottom of any page and tap Set an owner PIN.
  2. Choose 4 to 6 digits and enter your account password. That is the password you sign in with. Your team does not need to know it, which is what keeps them from setting a PIN of their own.
  3. To work on settings, tap Owner unlock at the top, or tap a locked action. Owner mode turns itself off after 10 minutes, or when you tap Lock.

If you forget it

In the PIN box, tap Forgot the PIN? and reset it with your account password. After five wrong tries the PIN locks for five minutes.

What it is, and is not.

The PIN stops accidental and casual changes. It is not a defense against someone determined with the device in hand.

Step 5 · Pro

Recipes

A recipe says what one drink uses. On the Recipes tab, type the menu item and tap Add, then + Add ingredient for each thing it uses. Amounts are in each item's own measure, so an ingredient needs an Amount per unit and a Measure first. The green line, like "~160 left to sell", shows how many you could still make from current stock.

Suggestion Name it like your register does.

When a recipe has the same name as the item at your register, sales match it automatically. Many shops start with their ten best sellers and add more as unmatched items show up.

Step 6 · Pro

Connect Square

  1. On the Recipes tab, tap Connect Square and sign in to your Square account when asked.
  2. Approve the two read-only permissions: view your orders and view your business profile. GroundWorks cannot change anything in Square.
  3. You land back on the Recipes tab. From here it checks for new sales about every ten minutes. Check for new sales does it right away.
  • Only sales after you connect count. Connecting never subtracts your past sales.
  • Matching: a sold item matches a recipe by name, ignoring capitals and spacing, or after a size or hot/iced word is removed. Anything else shows under "Sold in Square, no recipe yet". Pick the recipe it means once and it is remembered, or make a recipe with that name.
  • Never guessed: unmatched items deduct nothing. They wait in the list for you.
  • Disconnecting asks Square to remove GroundWorks from your authorized apps, and keeps your recipes and saved matches. If Square access is ever removed from Square's side, the card asks you to reconnect.
Your call.

You can connect Square and still count some items by hand (Setup C). Just keep a list of which is which.

Step 7 · Pro

Sizes and modifiers

Once sales start flowing, the sizes and add-ons your customers actually order appear under the Square card for you to set up. Nothing changes until you do: an unset size counts as 1×, and an unset modifier does nothing.

Sizes

Your recipe is the base size. Tell GroundWorks how much more or less each size uses, like 0.75 for a Small or 1.33 for a Large, and every drink sold in that size deducts that much. A recipe that already has the size in its name (like "Latte Large") is used as written.

Modifiers

For each add-on, choose what it does: no stock effect (like Extra Hot), uses an extra ingredient (Extra Shot adds 0.64 oz of beans), or swaps an ingredient (Oat Milk uses oat milk instead of whole milk, same amount).

Setup applies going forward.

Sales already counted keep the numbers they were counted with. Changing a size or modifier only affects sales that arrive afterward.

Step 8 · Pro

No Square? Use the end-of-shift import

If you use another register system, you can still deduct from sales without a connection. At the bottom of the Recipes tab, upload your item-sales report (a CSV file) or paste it. GroundWorks looks for the item name and quantity columns itself. If it cannot tell which columns they are, it asks you to pick them.

  • You see exactly what will change before anything does.
  • If you apply the same report twice, it warns you first.
  • Names you match once are remembered in that browser.
  • Reports from some systems may need a column picked the first time. Plain two-column lines like Vanilla Latte, 24 always work.
Step 9

Running the week

These are habits many shops settle into. Use the ones that help.

Closing count

Some shops do it every night. Shops with sales sync often do it weekly, since the sync keeps counts close. The count is quick when the numbers are already near right.

The Order tab

Check it before you place an order. The suggested amount gets you back to double the reorder level, so raising or lowering a level changes the suggestion. Mark items "Ordered ✓" so two people do not order twice, and use Share list to send it on.

Tune the levels

After two or three weeks, look at what hit Critical or never got low. Nudge those reorder levels. This one habit does the most for how useful the app feels.

Shared devices

If the shop tablet stays signed in, a PIN keeps settings separate. If your team uses personal phones, consider asking them to sign out when they are done.

Step 10

Plans and billing

Standard

Items and counts, the Order tab, text alerts, expiry dates, the owner PIN and the closing count.

Pro

Everything in Standard, plus recipes, the Square connection, sizes and modifiers, and the end-of-shift import.

Tap Manage billing at the bottom of any page to change plans, update a card or cancel. It opens a secure billing page. Current prices and the one-time setup fee are shown at checkout.
